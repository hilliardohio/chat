# Hilliard, OH — Per-District Development Standards
Source: City of Hilliard Code of Ordinances, Part Eleven (Planning & Zoning Code), Municode (jobId 485071). Chapters 1109 (Residential), 1111 (Business), 1113 (Industrial), 1115 (Special Purpose), 1117 (PUD). Verified against API content 2026-08-25. All setbacks/areas from Tables 1109-2/3/3a/3b, 1111-2/3/3a, 1113-2/3/3a, 1115-2/3/3a. "P" = permitted by right; "C" = conditional (Ch. 1123 approval).

Cross-cutting: every district chapter also points to Ch. 1121 (general provisions, incl. accessory buildings 1121.02(b), home occupations 1121.06(e)), Ch. 1123 (conditional uses), Ch. 1125 (landscaping), Ch. 1127 (parking), Ch. 1129 (signs), Ch. 1131 (site plan review).

## R-R — Rural Residential (Ch. 1109)
Purpose: single-family homes on large lots in rural areas without public utilities; protects farming and rural character (1109.01(a)).
Uses: detached single-family, parks P; farms, farm markets, kennels, riding stables, stables/greenhouses, cemeteries, schools, places of worship, group homes, bed & breakfast C (Table 1109-2). Home occupations and accessory buildings P.
Standards (Tables 1109-3/3a): min lot 100,000 sq ft, width 150 ft (non-res uses: 3 ac/250 ft); max height 35 ft; setbacks — front 40, side 15 least/35 total, rear 40; max lot coverage 25%; min floor area 1,800/2,000 sq ft (1-story/2-story).
Homeowner notes: only district allowing farm/animal uses; 60-ft setback from major-thoroughfare rights-of-way (1109.03 note 4).

## R-1 — Low Density Residential (Ch. 1109)
Purpose: create and sustain single-family neighborhoods served (or serviceable) by public water/sewer (1109.01(b)).
Uses: detached single-family, parks, home occupations, accessory buildings, Type-B day care home P; schools, worship, cemeteries, group homes, B&B, golf courses C (Table 1109-2).
Standards: min lot 13,500 sq ft, width 90 ft; lots without public water/sewer: 30,000 sq ft/150 ft (note 2). Max height 35 ft; front 35, side 12 least/30 total, rear 40; coverage 30%; min floor 1,600/2,000 sq ft (Tables 1109-3/3a).
Homeowner notes: corner lots need +10 ft width (1109.03(a)1); non-residential uses need 3 ac/250 ft.

## R-2 — Low/Medium Density Residential (Ch. 1109)
Purpose: single-family subdivision neighborhoods at modest densities, public water/sewer required (1109.01(c)).
Uses: same pattern as R-1 — detached single-family P; institutional uses (schools, worship, libraries, government) C (Table 1109-2).
Standards: min lot 11,200 sq ft, width 80 ft; max height 35 ft; front 25 (dwelling; garages incl. attached must sit back 35 ft — note 6), side 10 least/20 total, rear 35; coverage 30%; min floor 1,300/1,600 sq ft (Tables 1109-3/3a).
Homeowner notes: the 35-ft garage setback is unique to R-2; corner lots +10 ft width.

## R-3 — Moderate Density Residential (Ch. 1109)
Purpose: primarily single-family but allows a mix of dwelling types; transitional district near higher-intensity zones; public water/sewer required (1109.01(d)).
Uses: detached single-family P; two-family, attached single-family (townhouses), continuing-care retirement community, manufactured housing community, child day care center C (Table 1109-2).
Standards: min lot 10,000 sq ft/70 ft (two-family: 6,000 sq ft per unit/90 ft; townhouses: 10 units/ac max, 1-ac/200-ft site — note 3). Height 35 ft; front 30 (parking 20), side 10 least/20 total, rear 30; coverage 35%; min floor 1,200/1,500 sq ft.

## R-4 — High Density Residential (Ch. 1109)
Purpose: mix of residential unit types at higher densities; transition along thoroughfares/near shopping (1109.01(e)).
Uses: detached, attached single-family, multiple-family P; two-family P; boarding houses, nursing homes, CCRC C (Table 1109-2).
Standards: min lot 6,000 sq ft/60 ft (two-family 4,500 sq ft per unit/80 ft; townhouses 12 u/ac; multi-family 14 u/ac, 1-ac/200-ft site min — note 4). Height 35 ft (institutional/multi-family may exceed with +2 ft setback per extra ft, 70 ft absolute cap — note 1). Front 30 (parking 20), side 8 least/16 total, rear 30; coverage 35%; min floor 1,200/1,500 sq ft; multi-family floor minimums by bedrooms (600/750/900+).

## B-1 — Neighborhood Business (Ch. 1111)
Purpose: low-intensity, day-to-day convenience shopping/services for nearby residents; nodes of ≤5 acres, not strips (1111.01(a)).
Uses P: small restaurants (<2,000 sq ft, no drive-thru), personal services, medical/dental offices, banks, day care, dry cleaners, commercial studios. C: bars, general retail, microbrewery, short-term lending, upper-floor dwellings, drive-thru facilities (Table 1111-2).
Standards (Tables 1111-3/3a): min lot 7,500 sq ft/60 ft; height 35 ft; front 30 (parking and building), side 0, rear 20. Front building setback may drop 50% if no front-yard parking (1111.03 note 1).
Notes: smallest, most residential-compatible commercial district.

## B-2 — Community Business (Ch. 1111)
Purpose: larger, diversified community-wide retail/services along major thoroughfares; cohesive centers, shared access (1111.01(b)).
Uses P: general retail (any size), restaurants, bars, hotels, offices, health/fitness, government facilities, personal services, contractor-type commercial services. C: vehicle sales/repair/wash/service stations, drive-thru restaurants, outdoor recreation & storage, kennels, lumber yards, multi-family dwellings (Table 1111-2).
Standards: min lot 1 acre/200 ft; height 35 ft; front — parking 20, building 50; side 20; rear 30 (Table 1111-3a).

## B-3 — Office/Institutional (Ch. 1111)
Purpose: corporate/professional offices, research, institutions — an employment district, not retail; buffer between residential and intense commercial/industrial (1111.01(c)).
Uses P: corporate/professional offices, banks, medical labs & offices, data centers, research/testing labs, restaurants, training centers, printing, government buildings. C: hospitals, colleges, assembly halls, mortuaries (Table 1111-2). Most retail excluded.
Standards: min lot 1 acre/200 ft; height 45 ft; front — parking 20, building 50; side 20; rear 30 (Table 1111-3a).

## B-4 — I-270 Corridor Business (Ch. 1111)
Purpose: intense, high-quality mixed-use development combining offices with residential, neighborhood retail, and services along I-270 (1111.01(d)).
Uses P: offices, hotels, restaurants, retail <15,000 sq ft UFA, personal services, health facilities (C), banks, data centers. C: multi-family & attached single-family dwellings, grocery stores, drive-thru restaurants, indoor recreation, vehicle service stations (Table 1111-2).
Standards: min lot 1 acre/200 ft; height 70 ft; front — parking 40, building 60; side 30; rear 30; side/rear setbacks +1 ft per ft of height above 45 ft (Table 1111-3a note 2).
Note: 1111.04 adds special corridor design standards (pedestrian connectivity, building materials, orientation) on primary commercial corridors.

## M-1 — Restricted Industrial (Ch. 1113)
Purpose: light manufacturing/processing of previously refined materials with little/no impact on neighbors, plus supporting commercial (1113.01(a)).
Uses P: light manufacturing/assembly, tool & die, corporate offices, data centers, banks, research labs, food processing, trade schools, restaurants (no drive-thru), minor vehicle repair, commercial services. C: breweries, kennels, warehouses, self-storage, outdoor-storage utility yards, vehicle sales/major repair, recycling facilities, caretaker residence (Table 1113-2).
Standards (Tables 1113-3/3a): min lot 1 acre/100 ft; height 45 ft; front — parking 25, building 50; side 20; rear 40; coverage 30%.

## M-2 — General Industrial (Ch. 1113)
Purpose: larger-scale manufacturing, assembly, fabrication with potential external effects; sited near major roads/utilities (1113.01(b)).
Uses P: manufacturing/assembly, machine & metal shops, warehouses/distribution, mail-order fulfillment, data centers, produce terminals, utility facilities incl. outdoor storage. C: chemical manufacturing/storage, truck repair, recycling, mineral extraction, sexually oriented businesses (also C in M-1), caretaker residence (Table 1113-2).
Standards: min lot 2 acres/200 ft; height 45 ft; front — parking 25, building 50; side 30; rear 40; coverage 50% (Table 1113-3a). Setbacks also apply to quarries/excavation (note 1).

## S-1 — Support Facilities (Ch. 1115)
Purpose: public/semi-public uses needing large land areas — education, worship, recreation (1115.01(a)).
Uses: parks, pools, private open space, child care, community gardens (C) P/C mix; schools, places of worship, government facilities, libraries, private airfields, wireless facilities C (Table 1115-2). No dwellings.
Standards (Tables 1115-3/3a): min lot 3 acres/250 ft; height 45 ft/4 stories; front — parking 20, building 60; side 50 least/100 total; rear 60; coverage 20%.

## HCD — Hilliard Conservation District (Ch. 1115)
Purpose: implements the Big Darby Accord — conservation-style development protecting the Big Darby Creek watershed (1115.01(b)).
Uses: detached single-family P; two-family, attached single-family C; any B-1 use C; parks/open space P (Table 1115-2).
Standards (1115.04(e)): min tract 20 acres; NO minimum lot size; setbacks set case-by-case through development-plan review; at least 70% of gross acreage permanent open space, 50% "naturalized" open space; height 35 ft (Table 1115-3a). Conservation-subdivision design process per 1115.04(a)-(b).
Note: functions like a plan-reviewed district — standards fixed via the approved development plan.

## OH-MD — Old Hilliard Mixed Use (Ch. 1115)
Purpose: walkable downtown core mixing retail, dining, offices and housing; build-to continuity, rear parking, pedestrian realm (1115.01(c)).
Uses P: retail <15,000 sq ft, restaurants, bars, personal services, banks, offices, all dwelling types, B&B inns, short-term rentals. C: hotels, brew pubs, drive-thrus (pharmacy/bank only), larger retail/restaurants, upper-floor dwellings (Table 1115-2).
Standards: no min lot area/width; height 52 ft; build-to zone 0-10 ft front; side 0 (10 ft if not on lot line — note 2); rear 12 ft (Table 1115-3a).
Notes: Planning & Zoning Commission design review for exterior changes (1115.05); fences max 30 in., stockade prohibited; limited building-material palette; parking behind buildings/within 600 ft.

## OH-RD — Old Hilliard Residential (Ch. 1115)
Purpose: preserve/enhance the residential edge of downtown Old Hilliard with compatible small-lot housing (1115.01(c)).
Uses P: detached, attached, two-family, multi-family dwellings, B&B inns, short-term rentals, home occupations. C: retail, personal services, restaurants (small), CCRC, upper-floor dwellings (Table 1115-2).
Standards: min lot 7,000 sq ft/50 ft (60 ft corner); height 35 ft; front build-to zone 0-25 ft (or average of existing setbacks — note 3); side 5 least/12 total; rear 25; min floor 1,300 sq ft single-family, 900 two-family, multi-family by bedroom count (Table 1115-3a).
Notes: exterior modifications subject to P&Z Commission review; Old Hilliard fence/material standards apply (1115.05).

## PUD — Planned Unit Development (Ch. 1117)
How it works: a PUD is a negotiated zoning district — there is no fixed standards table. Council approves a development plan/text for each PUD; that approved text governs uses and standards. Baseline: each land-use type defaults to a reference district's standards (single/two-family → R-3; townhome/multi-family → R-4; retail/office → B-4; industry → M-1; institutional → S-1; Table 1117.04), but applicants may request written deviations (lot size, setbacks, height, coverage, signs, parking) approved by City Council with P&Z recommendation (1117.04(b)). Density bonuses possible (1117.04(c)). Minimum site: 20 ac residential, 40 ac mixed, 10 ac non-residential (Table 1117.02). Any use mix may be proposed (1117.03). Pre-2014 PUDs ("PUD*" on the zoning map) remain governed by their prior approved development plans (1117.09); "major changes" follow 1117.08 procedures. Practical answer for owners in a PUD: check your subdivision's approved PUD text with the Planning Division — citywide tables do not apply.
