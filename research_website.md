## 2. City Government: Council–Manager Form (no elected Mayor)

Hilliard uses a **Council–Manager form of government**: voters elect a seven-member City Council, and Council appoints a City Manager (by two-thirds vote) as chief administrative/executive officer. The City Manager oversees all departments, hiring/firing, implements the Council-approved budgets, and serves at the pleasure of Council. There is no resident recall of the City Manager. (There is a "Mayor's Court" — a traffic/misdemeanor court presided over by an appointed magistrate — but no elected mayor is listed on the site.)

## 4. Trash, Recycling & Yard Waste

- **Provider:** Local Waste Services (LWS), under city contract. Collection of trash, recycling AND yard waste is **every Tuesday** citywide (no zones).
- **Holiday rule:** If a holiday falls on Sunday, Monday or Tuesday, pickup slides to Wednesday; a Saturday holiday causes no change. 2026 examples: Memorial Day (Mon May 25) and Labor Day (Mon Sept 7) delay pickup; July 4 (Sat), Thanksgiving (Thu), Christmas (Fri) = no delay.
- **Bins:** Each residence gets a blue recycling bin and a green/gray trash bin (standard 65-gallon; damaged green bins replaced with gray). Larger 90-gallon recycling bin free from LWS at 614-409-9375; larger trash bin $65 through the City at 614-334-1209. Bin repair: LWS 614-409-9375 (not replaced for smell/filth).
- **Set up / transfer service & billing:** Local Waste Services 614-409-9375 (City trash line 614-334-1209: option 1 missed service, option 2 billing, option 3 start/cancel). Pay bill at https://www.localwasteservices.com/. Billed quarterly (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct). Unpaid bills can ultimately become a lien on the property.
- **Senior discounts:** through LWS — email ID + another utility bill to info@localwasteservices.com or fax 614-409-9376, or mail to 1300 S. Columbus Airport Road, Columbus, OH 43207. Financial hardship application available (PDF on the Trash page).
- **Missed pickup:** report to LWS 614-409-9375 option 2 within 24 hours.
- **Bulk items:** furniture, carpeting, mattresses/box springs collected with regular trash at the curb; upholstered items (incl. mattresses/box springs) **must be wrapped in plastic**. Large bulk pickups scheduled through LWS (localwasteservices.com).
- **Set-out rules:** containers at end of driveway by 7 a.m.; not in the street (on apron/tree lawn edge); max 50 lbs; recyclables loose (never bagged); return plastic grocery bags to stores; recyclables list at https://recycleright.org/recycle-right/. What-goes-where guidance: SWACO — https://www.swaco.org/.
- **Yard waste:** collected by LWS **March 1 – Nov 30, Tuesdays**, in biodegradable brown paper bags or rigid containers marked with free yard-waste stickers (available at 3800 Municipal Way). Brush bundles max 4 ft x 2 ft, branches ≤ 2 in diameter, ≤ 50 lbs per container. No food/pet waste, dirt, rocks, sod, plastic bags/pots. Christmas trees collected bare (no bags/decorations/tinsel).
- **Chipper service (City-run, on-demand):** for limbs/brush over 4 ft long and/or 4 in diameter; weekly on **Mondays, April 13 – Sept 24** (2026); submit a Hilliard Helps request by 2:30 p.m. the prior Friday; place material on tree lawn. Not accepted: logs >10 in diameter, firewood, vines, thorns, root balls, grass, materials with metal/nails/wire. (An older FAQ entry describes 2nd/4th-Friday chipper dates — the Yard Waste page schedule above is the current one.)
- **Extras:** GoZero food-waste composting program (https://hilliardohio.gov/gozero-community-composting-program/), Styrofoam recycling (https://hilliardohio.gov/styrofoam-recycling/).
- Sources: https://hilliardohio.gov/trash-recycling/ ; https://hilliardohio.gov/yard-waste/ ; https://hilliardohio.gov/questions-answers/ (Trash/Trees sections)

## 5. Water & Sewer Utilities

- **The City of Hilliard does not provide water/sewer service.** The **Columbus Division of Water** provides water and sewer service for Hilliard residents.
- **Start/stop service or billing questions:** Columbus Division of Water — start service page https://www.columbus.gov/Services/Public-Utilities/Start-New-Utility-Service; non-emergency requests **(614) 645-3111** or 311@columbus.gov (one FAQ answer also lists (614) 645-7788 for water-related questions).
- Water leaks, meter reading questions, temporary meters: Columbus Division of Water, same contacts.
- The City's Engineering Division manages storm and sanitary **sewer infrastructure** inside Hilliard; standing water/basement issues: usually homeowner responsibility unless caused by a city-owned line — call Hilliard 311, (614) 876-7361 ext. 311.
- **Other utilities:** Electricity — AEP Ohio lines/outages; the City's voter-approved green electric aggregation program is supplied by Dynegy Energy Services (https://hilliardohio.gov/aggregation/). Natural gas — Columbia Gas of Ohio. Ohio Energy Choice: energychoice.ohio.gov.
- Utility line marking before digging: Ohio Utilities Protection Service (OUPS), www.oups.org/edig, at least 48 hours before digging.
- Source: https://hilliardohio.gov/questions-answers/ (Utilities section)

## 6. Building Permits & Inspections

- **Apply online via the OpenGov portal** (formerly ViewPoint Cloud): https://hilliardoh.viewpointcloud.com/ — Building Standards category: https://hilliardoh.portal.opengov.com/categories/1079. All applications AND payments are handled through the portal. An application is not a permit — no construction until the permit is issued (minor interior demolition/discovery allowed in advance).
- **Common projects requiring permits:** basement finish, garages, room additions, decks, gazebos, satellite dishes, driveways, hot tubs, screened porches, electrical work, new home construction, sidewalk replacement, fences, patios, siding, fire/storm restoration, patio enclosures, storage sheds, fireplaces, plumbing, furnace replacement, roofing, swimming pools.
- **Review timeline:** most projects take about two weeks for plan and zoning review. Most permits are good for one year.
- **Engineering permits** (OpenGov Engineering category https://hilliardoh.viewpointcloud.com/categories/1084): sewer/water lateral replacement/repair; new or expanded driveway, approach or sidewalk.
- **Planning/Zoning review:** new buildings/additions require a Certificate of Zoning Compliance from the Planning Division (OpenGov category 1080).
- **Inspections:** required inspections list is provided with the issued permit through the portal.
- **Contractors:** every contractor working in Hilliard must be registered with the Building Department; homeowner is responsible for ensuring permits are filed. Homeowners may do their own work on their residence (not rentals); permits/inspections still required. Electrical permits required for any new/extended circuit; plumbing permits required.
- **Codes:** Ohio Building Code (codes.iccsafe.org/codes/ohio) and 2019 Residential Code of Ohio.
- Sidewalk repair is the adjacent property owner's responsibility (City repairs damage from City-tree roots, utility defects, and curb ramps). Driveway apron/gutter repair is the property owner's responsibility.
- Contact: Building@hilliardohio.gov; general help Hilliard 311.
- Source: https://hilliardohio.gov/building-standards-division/

## 10. Income Tax

- **Rate: 2.5%** (effective Jan 1, 2022).
- **Mandatory filing:** all Hilliard residents age 18+ must file a city return every year by **April 15** (next Monday if the 15th is a weekend), even if they owe nothing or work elsewhere. Exception: senior citizens with no income.
- **Administered/filed through RITA** (Regional Income Tax Agency, www.ritaohio.com) — RITA processes returns and collects tax; mail all returns/payments to RITA; checks payable to RITA.
- **Credit:** full credit for municipal tax paid to a work city, up to 2.5% (if work-city rate is lower, e.g. 2%, resident pays the difference).
- Taxable: earned income. Not taxable: retirement income, unemployment, certain disability income. Trusts are not taxed by the City.
- City Income Tax Division (Finance Dept.) at City Hall: **(614) 876-7361 ext. 778**, Tax1@hilliardohio.gov.
- Real estate/property taxes: Franklin County Auditor, (614) 525-4663, franklincountyauditor.com (not the City).
- Sources: https://hilliardohio.gov/taxes/ ; https://hilliardohio.gov/questions-answers/ (Taxes section) ; https://hilliardohio.gov/departments/finance/

## 11. Snow Removal, Street Maintenance & Leaf Collection

- **Snow:** City maintains hundreds of miles of roads (de-icing, plowing, salting) using a 5-priority system: (1) major arteries first, (2) minor arterials/connecting roads, (3) residential streets (generally not salted), (4) cul-de-sacs/courts/alleys if necessary (not salted), (5) city lots, sidewalks, curb ramps, paths. Residents: don't shovel snow into streets/aprons/around hydrants, remove parked cars, keep distance from plows. Residents are asked to keep their own sidewalks and driveway aprons clear.
- **Track plows live:** SnowPaths — https://portal.snowpaths.com/public/983278/983279 (also tracks leaf vacuums and chipper trucks).
- **Street maintenance:** annual repaving program prioritizes worst streets (Transportation & Mobility manages it); potholes handled by Operations — report via Hilliard 311, (614) 876-7361 ext. 311. Street sweeping roughly monthly per neighborhood, Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m., not in winter. City maintains traffic lights and streetlights within city limits (report via 311).
- **Leaf collection (curbside vacuum):** October through December, by color-coded neighborhood quadrant (Blue/Orange/Green/Purple; ~6 collection passes; 2026 schedule runs Oct 20 – Dec 19; stop raking to curb by Dec 14). Rake leaves to the tree lawn (between curb and sidewalk) by 7 a.m. Monday of the scheduled week — never into the street or around hydrants/drains. Zone map: PDF linked from the leaf page. Bagged leaves (biodegradable paper bags, ≤50 lbs) go with Tuesday yard-waste pickup through Nov 30.
- Sources: https://hilliardohio.gov/snowpaths/ ; https://hilliardohio.gov/leaf-collection/ ; https://hilliardohio.gov/questions-answers/ (Roads section)

## 13. Public Records Requests

- Page: https://hilliardohio.gov/public-records/
- No specific language, no writing, and no ID required to make a request (City may ask for a written request/identity only if it helps fulfill it, and must say writing is optional). Requests should identify records with reasonable clarity.
- **How to submit (general City records):** email **publicrecords@hilliardohio.gov**, or contact the **Clerk of Council, (614) 334-2365**, 3800 Municipal Way, Hilliard OH 43026 (Councilclerk1@hilliardohio.gov). Request form PDF and policy PDF are on the page.
- **Police records:** hpdrecordsunit@hilliardohio.gov (see section 9).
- Response: inspection "promptly," copies within a "reasonable" time; City aims to acknowledge in writing with clarification, time estimate, and cost estimate. Inspection during business hours 8 a.m.–5 p.m. weekdays. Redactions are noted; denials include legal authority.
- **Costs:** 5¢/page paper copies (8.5x11, 8.5x14, 11x17); $2/page large maps (black ink); emailed records free; other media at actual cost; no charge if copy cost under $20; postage may be charged in advance.
- New for 2026: under R.C. 9.66 (effective March 20, 2026), certain economic-development application/incentive records are confidential and not public records.
- Source: https://hilliardohio.gov/public-records/
