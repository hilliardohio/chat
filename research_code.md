## 1. Parking rules (street parking, RV/trailer parking)

**Code: Part Three (Traffic Code), Title Seven — Parking, Chapters 351–352**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTTHREETRCO_TITSEVENPA_CH351PAGE

- **§ 351.14 — Continuous parking (72-hour rule):** No vehicle may remain parked, standing, or abandoned on any street for a continuous period longer than **72 hours**.
- **§ 351.22(a) — Commercial vehicles:** On-street parking or standing of a **commercial vehicle for more than one hour is prohibited**, except while actively delivering/loading tools, materials, or merchandise, or buses/trucks conveying passengers to a public meeting, church, etc.
- **§ 351.22(b) — Recreational vehicles and trailers:** On-street parking or standing of a **recreational vehicle or trailer (as defined in § 301.48) for more than two hours is prohibited**, with the same tools-and-materials delivery exception. Violation is a minor misdemeanor (escalating with prior offenses within a year).
- **§ 351.03 — Prohibited standing or parking places:** standard list of locations where parking is prohibited (sidewalks, crosswalks, fire lanes, etc.); § 351.031 covers fire lanes specifically.
- **§§ 351.23–351.24 — Residential permit parking districts:** the City can designate residential permit districts where parking requires a permit.
- **No general citywide overnight parking ban was found in Chapter 351.** The 72-hour rule, the commercial/RV limits, and any posted/permit-district restrictions govern instead. (Verified against the full section list of Ch. 351; residents should still obey posted signs.)
- **Chapter 352 — Snow emergency regulations:** A "snow emergency" may be declared by the Mayor (or designee) when the weather service **forecasts 3 or more inches of snow** in a short period (§§ 352.01–352.02). Once declared and announced via local media, stopping/parking is prohibited on designated snow-emergency streets (§§ 352.03–352.05); vehicles left there can be cited/removed (§ 352.04).

## 2. Noise ordinance (quiet hours)

**Code: Chapter 531 — Noise Control (Part Five, General Offenses)**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTFIVEGEOFCO_CH531NOCO

- **§ 531.02 — Noise disturbances prohibited.** Quiet hours are effectively **10:00 p.m.–7:00 a.m.**: during those hours the code specifically prohibits (when they create a noise disturbance across a residential boundary): loading/unloading and handling of boxes, crates, containers, building materials, garbage cans; loudspeakers/PA systems (noncommercial and commercial); powered lawn/garden and similar outdoor equipment (snow-removal equipment exempted); and certain vehicle operations.
- **§ 531.03 — Maximum permissible sound levels by receiving land use.** For residential districts (including ND and PUD), the table lists **7:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.** with limits of 60/70 dBA and **10:00 p.m.–7:00 a.m.** with limits of 50/60 dBA. (Reading: 60 dBA daytime and 50 dBA nighttime as the basic residential limits, with 70/60 as the higher secondary column — exact column meaning UNVERIFIED; the day/night split at 10 p.m./7 a.m. is verified.)
- **§ 531.04** exemptions and **§ 531.07** special variances exist; **§ 531.99** penalty.
- **§ 509.08 — Hours of work (construction noise):** No exterior construction labor (excavating, carpentry, bricklaying, cement, stone work, etc. on buildings, utilities, streets) **on Sundays, or between 7:00 p.m. and 7:30 a.m.** on other days. Interior work allowed if it causes no noise/nuisance. Extensions can be requested in writing from the City Manager.

## 3. Animals (dog licensing, leash law, pet limits, chickens/livestock)

**Code: Chapter 505 — Animals and Fowl; Zoning §§ 1121.07–1121.09**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTFIVEGEOFCO_CH505ANFO

- **§ 505.06 — Pet limit:** **No more than four dogs or cats (any combination)**, excluding puppies/kittens under four months, per single-family dwelling (or per suite in a two- to four-family dwelling).
- **§ 505.02(c) — Leash/confinement (mirrors ORC 955.22):** Except when lawfully hunting, every dog owner must either (1) keep the dog **physically confined or restrained on the owner's premises** by leash, tether, adequate fence, supervision, or secure enclosure, or (2) keep it **under the reasonable control of some person**. Female dogs in heat must be leashed or kept on premises (§ 505.02(b)). Livestock may not run at large (§ 505.02(a), ORC 951.02).
- **§ 505.09 — Annual dog registration:** dogs must be registered annually per ORC 955.01 (in practice, the **Franklin County dog license**); § 505.10 dog tags; § 505.11 rabies immunization.
- **§ 505.14 — Livestock/fowl/bees prohibited:** No bees, reptiles, fowl, or livestock (horses, mules, cows, sheep, hogs, etc.) may be kept or run at large in the City **except as permitted by zoning §§ 1121.07–1121.09**.
- **Zoning § 1121.07(a):** household pets are a permitted accessory use in residential districts (repeats the 4 dog/cat cap for animals 6 months+). **Agricultural animals** (horses, cattle, goats, pigs, sheep) allowed only in the **R-R district on parcels of 3+ acres** (max 1 animal per gross acre on parcels of 10 acres or less; manure storage 75 ft from property lines).
- **§ 1121.08 — Backyard chickens:** Permitted in R-R, R-1, R-2, PUD, and HCD districts. **Lots under 0.5 acre: 0 chickens.** Lots 0.5–2.99 acres: up to **6**; 3–10 acres: 6 (R-1/R-2/PUD/HCD) or 3 per acre (R-R). Ducks/roosters count toward the max and only on 1+ acre lots. Requirements: predator-proof shelter (min 4 sq ft/chicken), confined yard (8 sq ft/chicken), no foraging forward of the house, **coops in rear yard only, at least 15 ft from all property lines**, not in easements; feed in rodent-proof containers; manure composting 25+ ft from side/rear lines; **zoning certificate required (§ 1141.03) and fence permit required** for the confined yard. Violation: minor misdemeanor.
- **§ 1121.09 — Rear yard beekeeping:** Permitted in same districts. Lots under 0.25 acre: 0 hives. 0.25–2.99 acres: up to 5 (R-R) or 3 (others); 3–10 acres: 2/acre (R-R) or 3. **6-ft flyway barrier** required where hives are within 30 ft of a property line; fence permit required for the barrier.

## 4. Property maintenance (grass height, weeds, junk vehicles)

**Code: Chapter 1303 (Property Maintenance Code), Chapter 917 (Weeds), § 1121.05, § 521.12**

- **§ 1303.01:** Hilliard has adopted the **2021 International Property Maintenance Code** (per Charter § 3.09), with local amendments in § 1303.02. One amendment sets the weed standard: *"Premises and exterior property shall be maintained free from weeds or plant growth in excess of 6 inches."*
  Source: ...?nodeId=PTTHIRTEENBULAUSCO_CH1303PRMACO
- **Chapter 917 — Weeds (Streets code):** § 917.02 — owners may not allow **weeds/rank vegetation over six (6) inches** or letting them seed. § 917.03 — written notice; must cut within **5 calendar days**. § 917.04 — if not, the City (March 1–Nov 30) may cut with City forces/contractors; § 917.05–.06 — costs billed to owner and, if unpaid in 30 days, **certified to the Franklin County Auditor as a lien** on the property. § 917.99 — 3rd-degree misdemeanor, **minimum $250 fine** (repeat: M2, min $500); each day a separate offense.
  Source: ...?nodeId=PTNINESTUTPUSECO_TITONESTSIAR_CH917WE
- **Junk/inoperable vehicles — Zoning § 1121.05:** It is unlawful to permit **open storage or parking of an inoperable motor vehicle**, machinery, equipment, or parts **outside an enclosed garage/building for more than 48 hours**. "Inoperable" includes vehicles that can't be driven under their own power or are unsafe/unable to comply with state law (e.g., dismantled or in disrepair). (Former Chapter 523 "Junk Vehicles" in the General Offenses Code was repealed; the zoning provision is the operative rule.)
- **§ 521.12 — Waste accumulation:** Accumulating garbage, trash, or debris (lumber, building materials, unused tires, vehicle parts, etc.) in a residential district is prohibited.

## 5. Fences, sheds, accessory structures

**Code: Zoning § 1121.02 (Buildings and Structures) and § 1121.03**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTELEVENPLZOCO_CH1121ZOGEPR

- **Fences — § 1121.02(d):**
  - **Permit required** before installing any new, replacement, or relocated fence/fence section (§ 1121.02(d)(2)); not required for regular maintenance of an approved fence. Application needs a survey/drawing showing property lines.
  - **No fences in front yards** (except invisible fences; corner-lot exception for aluminum/wrought-iron style up to 48 inches).
  - **Max height 6 feet** in side/rear yards in all districts except M-1/M-2 (7 ft); **chain-link fences in residential districts max 4 feet**. Fences may be placed on the property line.
  - Must be a **neutral color** (white, tan, light gray, light brown or similar); **finished side must face outward**; only one fence style per property line; bottom gap max 6 inches.
  - **Prohibited materials:** barbed wire, stockade, basket weave, electric, razor/concertina wire, solid plywood, scrap lumber, common concrete, cinderblock, and similar.
  - No encroachment on public easements; drainage-easement fences need City Engineer approval.
  - **Swimming pools (§ 1121.02(d)(5)):** all private pools must be fenced (4–6 ft) with self-latching, self-locking gates, unless equipped with a compliant ASTM F1346-91 locking safety cover.
  - Maintenance: fences must be kept structurally sound and finished; area around them clear of weeds/debris (§ 1121.02(d)(7)).
- **Clear vision corners — § 1121.03(c):** nothing over **30 inches** tall in the sight triangle at intersections (20-ft legs).
- **Accessory buildings (sheds, detached garages) — § 1121.02(b):**
  - Not allowed in any front yard or easement; allowed in **rear yard** with setbacks of at least **6 ft from rear** and **3 ft from side** property lines; may not occupy more than **30% of the required rear yard**.
  - Detached buildings must be 10+ ft from the principal building; attached/integrated ones follow principal-building setbacks.
  - **Max height 14 feet**; max size **50% of the principal building's floor area or 900 sq ft, whichever is less**.
  - No accessory building before the principal building exists; no separate utility metering; swing sets/playground equipment largely exempt except height limits.

## 6. Signs (yard sale, political)

**Code: Chapter 1129 — Signs (Zoning)**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTELEVENPLZOCO_CH1129SI

- **§ 1129.03 — Signs exempt from permit** include: **residential real-estate signs** (one per lot, max **8 sq ft**, max **4 ft high**, out of the right-of-way, removed within 7 days of closing); street-number signs; window signs (≤10% of window area, non-residential); **flags** (residential: one pole up to 20 ft); decorative/ornamental signs and banners without business names; **garage/yard sale signs — allowed without a permit but NOT in any public right-of-way and not on public infrastructure**.
- **§ 1129.04 — Prohibited locations:** no signs in any **public right-of-way** or easement, no-build zones, public parks, or other public property (without specific authorization), and none attached to traffic signs, utility poles, street signs, fences, or trees. Also prohibited: flashing/rotating signs, roof signs, billboards.
- **Political signs:** the chapter has **no separate political/election sign category** (post-*Reed v. Town of Gilbert* content-neutral drafting). Political yard signs fall under the general temporary/exempt-sign and location rules above — key practical rule: keep them on private property, out of the right-of-way. (Absence of a category verified from the full chapter section list and keyword search.)
- **§ 1129.05 — Temporary signs (permit required):** max **six two-week periods per calendar year**; temporary banners need a permit, max 40 sq ft, attached to building/ground, not on trees or roofs, outside the right-of-way.
- **Garage sales themselves — Chapter 727 (Business Code):** max **two garage sales per residence per 12 months**, max **3 consecutive days**, hours **9 a.m.–6 p.m. (Oct 1–Mar 31)** and **9 a.m.–8 p.m. (Apr 1–Sep 30)** (§§ 727.01–.02). Signs must comply with Ch. 1129 (§ 727.03). Violation: minor misdemeanor (§ 727.99).

## 7. Fireworks

**Code: Chapter 1519 — Fireworks (Part Fifteen, Fire Prevention Code)**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTFIFTEENFIPRCO_CH1519FI

- **§ 1519.04(a):** No person may **possess or sell fireworks** in the City except licensed manufacturers, wholesalers, shipping-permit holders, out-of-state residents (per ORC 3743), and licensed exhibitors.
- **§ 1519.04(b):** Except for a licensed exhibitor conducting a permitted public exhibition (ORC 3743.50–.55 and § 1519.02), **no person shall discharge, ignite, or explode any fireworks in the City** — i.e., Hilliard prohibits consumer (backyard) fireworks discharge, unlike Ohio's default holiday-discharge law.
- **§ 1519.02:** public fireworks exhibitions require a City permit, fee, and bond.
- **§ 1519.99 — Penalty:** first-degree misdemeanor for a first offense, fine up to **$1,000**.

## 8. Curfew for minors

**Code: Chapter 539 — Offenses Relating to Minors**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTFIVEGEOFCO_CH539OFREMI

- **§ 539.01 — Under age 12:** may not loiter/idle/wander/play in streets, public places, vacant lots, etc. **from darkness to dawn**.
- **§ 539.02 — Under age 18:** same prohibition **between 12:00 midnight and 6:00 a.m.**
- **Exceptions (both):** accompanied by parent/guardian/custodial adult; emergency errand or legitimate business directed by the parent/guardian; (under-18 only) newspaper delivery, or traveling directly to/from employment.
- **§ 539.03 — Parents:** first offense = warning; second = minor-misdemeanor fine; third+ = 4th-degree misdemeanor.

## 9. Home occupations / business licensing

**Code: Zoning § 1121.06(e); Residential use table § 1109.02; Business Regulation Code (Part Seven)**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTELEVENPLZOCO_CH1121ZOGEPR

- **Home occupations are permitted accessory uses in all residential districts** (§ 1109.02 schedule of uses), subject to § 1121.06(e):
  - **Permit required** from the Planning Director before starting (fee set by Council).
  - Must be operated **entirely within the principal dwelling**; **only residents** of the dwelling may work in it.
  - Max **25% of gross floor area or 250 sq ft, whichever is less**.
  - **No visible change** to the dwelling's exterior; one non-illuminated wall sign up to 2 sq ft allowed.
  - **No on-premises retail sales** to customers (phone/internet sales OK if shipped directly); no outdoor storage or displays; no more traffic than normal for the neighborhood; off-street parking behind the front setback; no off-premises noise, vibration, glare, fumes, odor, or electrical interference.
- **Other business licensing (Part Seven):** Chapter 729 — registration of contractors; Chapter 745 — canvassers, peddlers, and solicitors; Chapter 753 — mobile food vending; Chapter 709 — alarm systems; Chapter 757 — tobacco retail licenses. (General licensing Ch. 705 repealed.)

## 10. Snow/ice sidewalk clearing responsibility

**Code: Chapter 909 — Sidewalks, Curb Ramps, and Shared-Use Paths**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTNINESTUTPUSECO_TITONESTSIAR_CH909SICURASHEPA

- **§ 909.02 — Responsibility:** Abutting **property owners are responsible for maintenance, repair, and replacement of existing sidewalks** in the right-of-way (the City repairs curb ramps).
- **§ 909.02(i) — Routine maintenance and care:** *"Property owners are responsible for the routine maintenance and care of sidewalks, curb ramps, and shared-use paths at all locations where the property is adjacent to the public right-of-way. **This includes removal of snow, ice**, vegetation, and debris."* (Ord. 22-45; Ord. 25-20, 8-25-25.)

## 11. Trash container rules

**Code: Chapter 975 — Solid Waste Collection**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTNINESTUTPUSECO_TITFIVEOTPUSE_CH975SOWACO

- **§ 975.02:** Solid waste may not be kept at a household **longer than 14 days**. Backyard composting allowed if no odor/litter/vermin nuisance.
- **§ 975.05 — Container rules:**
  - Householders must maintain an approved container.
  - **Storage:** containers must be kept **behind the house, or beside it but not forward of the front face of the dwelling**, or in an enclosed area not forward of the build line.
  - **Set-out:** no earlier than **5:00 p.m. the day before** collection day.
  - **Bring-in:** containers must be removed from the curb/driveway/streetlawn **by 9:00 a.m. the morning after** collection day.
  - Containers may only be placed for collection in front of the householder's own property.
  - Holiday weeks: collection shifts to the following day.
- **§ 975.06 — Rates:** households are billed 100% of actual monthly cost; **10% senior discount** for resident owners 65+.
- **§ 975.03:** dumping/burying/burning solid waste in (or within 5,000 ft of) the City is prohibited.
- **§ 975.99:** violations are minor misdemeanors (each day separate); service can be terminated for delinquency.

## 12. Zoning districts overview

**Code: Chapter 1104 — Zoning Districts and Maps**
Source: https://library.municode.com/oh/hilliard/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTELEVENPLZOCO_CH1104ZODIMA

- **§ 1104.01:** The City has **16 zoning districts** (Table 1104-1):
  - **Residential (Ch. 1109):** R-R Rural Residential; R-1 Low Density; R-2 Low/Moderate Density; R-3 Moderate Density; R-4 High Density
  - **Business (Ch. 1111):** B-1 Neighborhood Business; B-2 Community Business; B-3 Office/Institutional; B-4 I-270 Corridor
  - **Industrial (Ch. 1113):** M-1 Restricted Industrial; M-2 General Industrial
  - **Special Purpose (Ch. 1115):** S-1 Support Facilities; HCD Hilliard Conservation District; OH-MD Old Hilliard Mixed-Use; OH-RD Old Hilliard Mixed-Use Residential
  - **Planned (Ch. 1117):** PUD Planned Development
- **§ 1104.02:** Official Zoning District Map kept by the Planning Director; amendments per Ch. 1139.
- **§ 1104.05:** a lot split by a district line is treated as wholly in the more restrictive district.
- **§ 1104.06:** uses not listed in a district are not permitted there (similar-use determination per § 1121.06(h)).
- Administration/enforcement: Ch. 1141 (zoning certificates § 1141.03; penalties § 1141.06). Board of Zoning Appeals: Ch. 1106.
